## Full description (2700 / 4000 chars)

```
OTDA is a fast, offline-first task manager for OpenTodo servers — your tasks, your server, your data.

Point it at your OpenTodo instance (v1.5.0 or later), sign in with your password or an API token, and everything you already have is there: projects, labels, priorities, due dates, repeats and notes. A change made on your phone, on the web or on another device converges the next time they sync, even if one of them was offline for a week.

WRITE TASKS THE WAY YOU THINK
Type a whole task in one line: "Write report tomorrow 3pm #work @call p2 +45m". OTDA reads the date, the time, the project, the label, the priority and the estimate as you type, shows what it understood right under the input, and lets you correct any part with a tap. It understands English, German, French, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese and Dutch, including phrases like "every other Friday", "in 2 weeks", "remind me 30 min before" and "deadline Friday".

REPEATING TASKS THAT BEHAVE
Ticking off an occurrence does not end the series: a daily task finished today comes back tomorrow. Skip a single occurrence, or delete only this one, everything from here on, or the entire series — each means something different. Finished occurrences stay in History.

TODAY, UPCOMING, CALENDAR
A Today list that knows what is due or has already started, an Upcoming list that projects a repeat onto its next occurrence, a month calendar that shows every occurrence on the day it falls, and search that puts the newest first.

REMINDERS THAT ACTUALLY FIRE
Local notifications armed through the system alarm clock, with Complete, Snooze 10 minutes and Tomorrow on the notification itself — no need to open the app. A test reminder and a status card in Settings show whether your phone is going to let them through.

A WIDGET THAT CANNOT DISAGREE WITH THE APP
A home-screen widget grouped by Overdue, Today, Tomorrow, This week and Later, built from the same code as the lists, so it always matches. Tick a task off straight from the widget.

MADE FOR PEOPLE WHO RUN THEIR OWN SERVER
Workspaces, members and roles, project colours and icons, profile pictures. If you own the server or are an admin, the app also carries the admin tools: users and roles, invitations, the audit log, backups and storage.

LOOKS THE WAY YOU WANT
AMOLED black, light or dark, your system font or the bundled Allo, and a layout that stays readable when it is small.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no account with us. Your data goes only to the server you configure and we never see it. The app is open source.

OTDA is a client, not a service: you need an OpenTodo server to sync with. Without one it still works offline on a single device.
```
