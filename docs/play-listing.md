## Full description (3988 / 4000 chars)

```
OTDA is a fast, offline-first task manager for OpenTodo servers — your tasks, your server, your data.

Point it at your OpenTodo instance (v1.5.0 or later) and everything you already have is there: projects, labels, priorities, dates, repeats and notes. Changes from your phone, the web or another device converge on the next sync, even after a week offline.

WRITE TASKS THE WAY YOU THINK
Type a whole task in one line: "Write report tomorrow 3pm #work @call p2 +45m". OTDA reads the date, the time, the project, the label, the priority and the estimate as you type, shows what it understood, and lets you correct any part with a tap. It understands English, German, French, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese and Dutch, including phrases like "every other Friday", "in 2 weeks", "remind me 30 min before" and "deadline Friday".

REPEATING TASKS THAT BEHAVE
Ticking off an occurrence does not end the series: a daily task finished today comes back tomorrow. Skip a single occurrence, or delete only this one, everything from here on, or the entire series — each means something different. Finished occurrences stay in History.

TODAY, UPCOMING, CALENDAR
A Today list of what is due or has started, an Upcoming list that projects a repeat onto its next occurrence and ends with everything that has no date, a month calendar of every occurrence, and search that puts the newest first.

SUBTASKS THAT STAY IN ORDER
Break a task into subtasks, move one under another or promote it. Children sit under their parent with a completed/total count; folding is remembered per device. Completing a parent completes open subtasks; completing the last child offers to complete the parent.

MORE THAN A TITLE
Set a deadline, a start date or Someday, an estimate, an energy level, a section and labels without leaving the task sheet. Every edit is a field write, so two devices editing different fields do not overwrite each other.

TRASH, STATISTICS AND HISTORY
Deleted tasks and projects wait in a Trash you can restore from, or purge for good. Statistics show completions per day, week, project and label, plus streaks for repeating tasks. The activity feed covers shared projects, and every task keeps its own change history.

YOUR DATA, IN AND OUT
Export the account as a ZIP (with files) or JSON and share it through the system sheet; an encrypted export is ciphertext plus the wrapped key record, and the app says so.

REMINDERS THAT ACTUALLY FIRE
Local notifications armed through the system alarm clock, with Complete, Snooze 10 minutes and Tomorrow on the notification itself — no need to open the app. A test reminder and a status card in Settings show whether your phone is going to let them through.

A WIDGET THAT CANNOT DISAGREE WITH THE APP
A home-screen widget grouped by Overdue, Today, Tomorrow, This week, Later and Anytime, built from the same code as the lists, so it always matches. Tick a task off or refresh straight from the widget.

MADE FOR PEOPLE WHO RUN THEIR OWN SERVER
Switch between the workspaces you belong to; project colours, icons and profile pictures. Owners and admins also get users and roles, invitations, the audit log, backups and storage.

LOOKS THE WAY YOU WANT
System, light or dark, with a separately chosen dark grey or AMOLED black; an accent colour; comfortable or compact density; and a row that shows everything, dates only or just the title. Instance themes become the default palette.

SECURE SIGN-IN
Password or API token, plus two-factor authentication set up from the app: enrol an authenticator, save recovery codes once, see remembered browsers and turn it off. Sensitive changes ask for the password again.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no account with us. Your data goes only to the server you configure and we never see it. The app is open source.

OTDA is a client, not a service: you need an OpenTodo server to sync with. Without one it still works offline on a single device.
```
